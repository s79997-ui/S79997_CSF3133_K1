About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and research.

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the centre was transformed into a branch campus, being named Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996. Later, the cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001, before it was officially renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007.
