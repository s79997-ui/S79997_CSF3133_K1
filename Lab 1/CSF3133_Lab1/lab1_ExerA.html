How to Order

  1. Find an available table and scan the QR code located on the corner.
  2. Browse our digital menu and add your favorite items to the cart.
  3. Proceed to checkout and pay online via DuitNow QR or card.
  4. Collect your freshly made order at the pick-up counter when your number is called.
